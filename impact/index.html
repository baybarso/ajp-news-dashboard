Methodology & disclosures

Scope. 39 newsrooms in the American Journalism Project portfolio. The list of outlets is fixed for this dashboard and is updated only when AJP’s public portfolio changes.

Date filter. Only reports labeled 2024, 2025, or 2026 are shown. Older impact reports the outlets may have published (e.g., 2021–2023 annual reports) are intentionally excluded from this view, even if they still exist on the outlet’s site. Undated permanent “Our Impact” landing pages, where they exist, are included regardless of age because they typically aggregate current and historical impact.

What counts as an impact report. Two things: (1) formal dated reports — multi-page annual reports, quarterly reports, year-in-review documents, donor/impact reports with a clear fiscal-period label, in PDF or HTML; and (2) permanent impact landing pages — pages titled “Impact”, “Our Impact”, or “About → Impact” that summarize how the outlet’s reporting has affected policy or community outcomes. Individual story-level impact updates, donor acknowledgment pages, investigative series titled “Impact” without a structured summary wrapper, and press releases announcing impact reports do not qualify.

How outlets are scanned. Each outlet is queried via web search using two to three site-restricted queries per outlet (combinations of site:domain “impact report”, “annual report”, “year in review”, “our impact”, and filetype:pdf). Results are filtered by hand against the definitions above. When more than three qualifying reports are found, the three most recent are shown and the rest are tucked into “earlier reports” expanders.

NEW detection. Each scan compares discovered report URLs against the previous scan’s state.json. URLs not in the prior set are marked NEW. The first scan establishes the baseline and carries no NEW flags.

Refresh cadence. On-demand only — this dashboard refreshes when a user explicitly runs the scan. It is not on a recurring schedule, so the “Last scanned” timestamp at the top reflects the most recent manual run.

Limitations. Coverage depends on what web search indexes for each outlet’s domain; some impact reports embedded in newsletters, gated donor portals, or PDF-only on subdomains may not surface. Where an outlet has not published a dated report but maintains a permanent impact page, that page is captured so the row still appears as indexed. Outlets with neither a dated 2024–2026 report nor a permanent impact page sink to the bottom of the list under “No impact reports indexed yet.”
